How it works

Three steps, ten seconds

You keep control of the idea. The commands add the camera, light and style wording that image tools respond to.

1

Write your idea

Say what you want, then add commands such as /goldenhour or /85mm. Plain words like "cinematic" work too.

2

Pick your tool

Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Flux, DALL-E, Gemini, Google Flow or Kling. The prompt is formatted the way that tool likes it.

3

Copy and paste

One click copies the finished prompt, plus a negative prompt when your tool has one. A list shows what every command added.
